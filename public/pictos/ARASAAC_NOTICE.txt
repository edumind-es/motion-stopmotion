This app includes a local example catalog (JSON) that references labels and keywords only.
To use ARASAAC pictograms offline, download the required images from ARASAAC and place them
under: /static/pictos/arasaac/<category>/<id>.png (or .svg), and add an "img" property in the
catalog entries pointing to your local file. Example:

  { "id":"feliz", "label":"feliz", "img":"/static/pictos/arasaac/emociones/feliz.png", "keywords":[...] }

Make sure to keep attribution and comply with the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.
